# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- A black-and-white manga page: white ink on solid black, screentone for shading, and one crimson-pink kept for ratings, progress, the favourite and the current page.
- Cards are square manga panels, the picture and the caption split by a black gutter. On hover the frame thickens and speed lines (scenes) or focus lines (performers and images) rush in for a beat, with an SFX: ジー, ニヤ or チュッ. Titles stop at two lines and the counts sit level along a row.
- The rating is a red caption cut on the slant, to the right of the card's checkbox, and the studio a paper narration box.
- Inked stars for ratings that catch a glint now and then, a heart for the favourite that pounds and pops ドキッ on hover, keyboard focus and click (a tap on a phone flashes it once), and the anime sweat drop for the O counter.
- Inked buttons with a screentone shadow that lift on hover and land on a press; the list toolbar and the pager stay flat.
- Toasts and tooltips are speech bubbles, with フン over every toast. Menus are panels with a tone shadow, and modals carry ゴゴゴ in the corner.
- A performer's page opens as a two-panel spread: her picture in a slanted panel, her name lettered across the gutter, ドン breaking the frame and her details in a paper narration box. With Stash's Enable background image setting on, her blurred picture shows through the gutters in grey.
- The Tagger stays flat and still. Its verdicts are tick, bang and cross boxes in black, white and red, and the scrape dialog turns the winning side's toggle paper.
- A loader of focus lines spinning in held frames with ゴゴゴ, and an empty list that falls silent: her panel, a …… bubble and ぷい, then a line.
- Tsundere copy for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Sparkle, star, streak and sweat-drop bursts on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the ink and drops the voice, the SFX and the figure.
- Dela Gothic One, Caveat Brush and Zen Kaku Gothic New bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts. Dela Gothic One's Japanese ships as a 2 KB subset holding only the characters the SFX letter.
