# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release: Overclock as a style for Stash UI.

- Overclock joins the presets under Stash UI's Settings > Appearance > Colors.
- It gives Stash UI Overclock's colours and type. Page titles turn to yellow capitals with a glitch split, tags and readouts to a monospace, and a reticle sits by the wordmark.
- Its dot grid, haze and scanline replace Stash UI's halftone screen. Stash UI's own background image takes its place when it is on.
- Colors only, off by default, keeps Stash UI's own fonts, lettering and backdrop. It shows under the presets while Overclock is picked, and under Settings > Plugins.
- Tested beside Stash UI 3.115.1.
