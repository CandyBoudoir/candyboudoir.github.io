Ukiyo

the floating world

An Edo night print: Prussian-blue ground graded to black, indigo washi, cream cartouches and vermilion, with whatever is chosen printed a little out of register. Ratings are cherry blossoms, the favourite is a red seal carved with 恋, and the O counter is a breaking wave.

Palette

Pigments a woodblock printer kept: Prussian blue (bero-ai) graded to near black as the night sky, indigo for the paper, kinari cream for cartouches, vermilion (shu) for what is chosen, cherry pink and ochre as small accents. Text is kinari on indigo at 13.5:1, secondary text 7.7:1 and dates 5.8:1.

Type

Shippori Mincho B1, a mincho drawn after old letterpress, carries the wordmark, titles and her lines. Zen Kaku Gothic New in letterspaced caps carries buttons, tags and labels. Both ship inside the plugin, Latin only: the two Japanese words the theme prints, 浮世 and 恋, are carved into its seals as outlines, so no Japanese font ships at all.

Motifs and glyphs

Six motifs drawn as a printer cuts them: flat colour inside a sumi keyline. The breaking wave with clawed foam, the cherry blossom, the folding fan (sensu) with a cream moon, the paper lantern (chōchin), the red seal carved with 恋, and the moon behind mist. The paper under everything is washi, its long kōzo fibres laid in soft, and the keylines on cards and pictures are cut by hand. Below are the glyphs the controls use.

Buttons

Buttons are flat blocks of colour inside a sumi keyline. On hover the colour block slips out of register, a few pixels off its keyline, the way a misaligned block prints. Whatever is chosen stays out of register at rest: the page in the navbar, the chosen button of a group, the current page of a list, a focused field and the chosen Tagger row. Primary is vermilion, secondary indigo, and the others are grass green, ochre, Prussian blue and deep beni red. Button keylines stay plain and even.

Tags, ratings and favourites

Tags are indigo slips with a vermilion dot. A rating is a row of cherry blossoms, empty ones printed as keyline only. The favourite is a red seal carved with 恋 (koi, romantic love), a letter sealed in red, and it stamps itself on; the O counter is a breaking wave. Click them and petals, foam and flecks of mica scatter and drift down.

Cards

Cards are indigo washi with the picture framed like a print on its paper. Both keylines are cut by hand: they swell and thin along their length and soften at the corners. A scene title is plain display type at rest and prints its cream cartouche on hover, so a grid of forty stays calm; a performer's name keeps its cartouche, as a bijin-ga names its sitter. On hover a vermilion block slides out of register behind the card. Ratings hang on a vermilion tanzaku, the slip a poem is written on, to the right of the card's checkbox.

Performer page

Stash's pages have no hero, so the art lives here. Her header is a night print: a bokashi sky with the moon and wave scales (seigaiha) at its foot. If Stash's own background-image setting is on, her blurred picture stays only as a faint tone in the sky, so any picture works. Her picture is framed on its paper with a hand-cut keyline and a vermilion block out of register, and her name sits in a cream cartouche, as a bijin-ga names its sitter. Her details are a ruled ledger, and tab counts are small blocks, the chosen one in vermilion.

Forms and menus

Fields are sunk into the indigo. Focus prints the field out of register: its keyline turns to paper and a vermilion block lands off to the lower right. Switches carry the moon across a red sky. Menus and modals are washi with a faint hemp-leaf (asanoha) pattern; tooltips and toasts are cream slips with a keyline and a seal, and a modal's title sits in a cartouche over a band of wave scales.

In a scrape dialog the winning side's toggle is printed in vermilion. StashIDs carry a cream cartouche.

Navigation

The navbar is the bokashi band across the top of a print, dark at its edge and blue at its foot, with the lantern row from the night print at its right end and wave scales along its foot. The active page is a vermilion block printed out of register. On a phone held upright Stash docks the bar at the bottom, and the waves move to its top edge. The chosen tab is underlined in vermilion, and in the pager the current page is printed out of register too.

Copy

Formal, unhurried and a little haughty: the oiran's voice, taken from the Yoshiwara's real customs and never from her costume. She does not speak at a first meeting (shokai), accepts a patron only on his third visit (najimi), refuses whom she pleases and seals her letters in red; the rest is the printer's craft, blocks cut, ink drying, a print sealed. Matches are exact and whole-text, so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched. Plain labels and Stash's SFW mode turn them off.

Stash says Ukiyo says

Shippori Mincho B1 by FONTDASU and Zen Kaku Gothic New by Yoshimichi Ohira, both SIL OFL 1.1 and bundled with the plugin, since Stash blocks font hosts; the kanji on the seals are outlines drawn from Yuji Syuku by Kinuta Font Factory (SIL OFL 1.1). The night print and the beauty print were rendered locally with Qwen-Image 2512 (Apache 2.0). Bootstrap 4.6 stands in for Stash's own styles. Built with Claude, Anthropic's AI coding assistant.
