# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- An Edo woodblock night print: a Prussian-blue sky graded to black (bokashi), indigo washi paper, cream cartouches with sumi keylines and vermilion for what is chosen. The sea from the night print lies faintly along the foot of the page.
- Whatever is chosen is printed a little out of register: the active page in the navbar, the chosen button of a group, the current page in the pager, a focused field and the chosen Tagger row, which never moves.
- The navbar is a bokashi band with the lantern row from the night print at its right end and wave scales (seigaiha) along its foot, and the wordmark sits in a cream cartouche signed with a 浮世 seal. On a phone held upright the waves run along the top of Stash's bottom bar.
- Cards are washi with the picture framed like a print on its paper, both keylines cut by hand. Scene titles are plain until you point at one, then print their cartouche; performer names keep theirs. The rating hangs on a vermilion tanzaku to the right of the card's checkbox.
- Cherry blossoms for ratings, a red seal carved with 恋 for the favourite, which stamps itself on, and a breaking wave for the O counter.
- The performer header is a night print: the moon, the wave scales and her picture on its paper with a vermilion block behind it, her name in a cartouche. Stash's own Enable background image setting still decides whether her picture tones the sky.
- The loader is the night canal from the print, with a swaying lantern, a falling petal and moonlight on the water. An empty list shows the beauty print (bijin-ga) beside her line.
- The oiran's copy for a short list of Stash's messages, taken from the Yoshiwara's real customs and the printer's craft, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Petal, blossom, foam and mica bursts on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the print and drops the voice and the beauty print.
- Shippori Mincho B1 and Zen Kaku Gothic New bundled in the plugin, Latin only, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts; the kanji on the seals are SVG outlines, so no Japanese font ships. The washi is a seamless lossless tile at 1x, 2x and 3x, so every screen gets its own pixels.
