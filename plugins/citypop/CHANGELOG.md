# Changelog

## 0.2.5

Performers Toolkit 0.22 on a performer's page, for Stash v0.31.1.

- A performer's header is an open J-card again when Performers Toolkit tidies it, with the restyle on or off. Performers Toolkit 0.22 ran the rows after the name across the spine's column, so Stash's buttons, the toolkit's strip and its facts at a glance started under the spine and the cover's shadow, and the Edit button was hidden. It also stretched the cover taller than the spine. The rows now stay on the flap, the cover keeps its 2:3 and the spine its height, and Stash's buttons sit at the foot of the J-card.
- Under 1200px wide, the toolkit's Body panel lies under the J-card in line with the cover and clear of its shadow.
- Tested beside Insights 2.14.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.22.0.

## 0.2.4

Settings read from Stash's own copy, for Stash v0.31.1.

- City Pop reads its settings from the copy of Stash's configuration that Stash's own pages hold, and no longer asks Stash for them on every page. On Stash v0.30.0 to v0.31.1 a configuration query that meets a configuration write can freeze Stash until it restarts, so this lowers the risk of a freeze. A setting changed in Settings › Plugins still takes effect on the next page, and one changed in another browser at the next reload.
- Tested beside Insights 2.13.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.19.0.

## 0.2.3

Cherry Pick 0.3.1's bracket dialogs in the dusk, for Stash v0.31.1.

- The speaker in a bracket's header reads with sound on. City Pop paints a dialog's header icons coral, and the speaker's coral sat on the sunset of the button turned on. An icon on a header button now takes the button's ink and the height of the map size marks beside it.
- The speaker keeps one width with sound on or off, so the map size buttons beside it stay put.
- Tested beside Insights 2.12.1 and Performers Toolkit 0.17.1.

## 0.2.2

Performers Toolkit 0.17 on a performer's page, for Stash v0.31.1.

- A performer's details read again when Performers Toolkit tidies the header. The toolkit sets them out as a grid with each name over its value, and City Pop's track listing squeezed each value to a letter a line. With the restyle on or off, the details now follow the toolkit's grid. With Restyle Performers Toolkit on, the names, the toolkit's facts at a glance and its Also row are in City Pop's spaced capitals.
- The cover meets the spine again when Performers Toolkit 0.17 shows its Body panel beside the header, with the restyle on or off.
- Tested beside Insights 2.12.1 and Performers Toolkit 0.17.1.

## 0.2.1

Insights 2.12 and Performers Toolkit 0.13 in the dusk, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Insights 2.12 and Performers Toolkit 0.13 pick up City Pop's colours by themselves, so with the restyle settings off they already sit in its navy.
- With Restyle Insights on, Insights' calendars and its codec and resolution colours take City Pop's palette too, and empty days sit in night blue.
- With Restyle Performers Toolkit on, the toolkit's new Stats tab is in the dusk: its figures and charts are navy panels on the hard shadow, with their titles and numbers in Jost. Its Stats tab before 0.13 is no longer restyled.
- A performer's page stays an open J-card when Performers Toolkit tidies the header or adds its full-body picture panel, with the restyle on or off. Before, the name strip came away from the cover.
- Tested beside Insights 2.12.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.13.1.

## 0.2.0

Insights and Performers Toolkit in the dusk, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Restyle Insights and Restyle Performers Toolkit settings put those two plugins by another author in City Pop's look: navy panels on the hard shadow, their names in the brush script, Stash's tabs with the sunset under the open one, enamel buttons, night-blue fields and chips with the chosen one in sunset, pool-water switches, coral links, a cream tooltip, and sheets and a toast under the stripe band. Each tab's colour comes from City Pop's palette. Insights' heatmaps and its codec, resolution and chart colours keep their own tones.
- Both settings start off, and each shows in Settings > Plugins only while its plugin is on. A change takes effect on the next page you open, without a reload, and so do Calm mode and Plain labels.
- Insights' What works sidebar clears City Pop's taller navbar, and the count on Performers Toolkit's Catalog tab keeps City Pop's tab colours, with the restyle on or off.
- Tested beside Insights 2.11.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.4.2.

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- An airbrushed 1980s dusk after Japanese city pop album covers: a night-blue sky graded into violet with stars, palm silhouettes and a sunset glow, deep navy panels, sunset coral and pool turquoise.
- A striped sun low in the corner that stays down wherever a list, a detail page or the player covers the horizon, so it never sits behind cards, and rises in Settings.
- The navbar carries the J-card stripe band along its foot, with the active page as a sunset sticker and the brand in chrome script. On a phone held upright the band runs along the top of Stash's bottom bar.
- Cards are record sleeves with a printed keyline and a pressed crease. Hover lifts the sleeve, sweeps a sunset across the picture, flashes a glint and shows the catalogue label. The rating is a cream cassette-label sticker beside the checkbox, Cherry Pick's Your score is the same sticker left blank in night blue with a dashed edge, and progress is an FM dial.
- Audio-only scenes with no cover of their own are a cassette on a pool at dusk, found with one GraphQL request per batch of new cards.
- A performer's page is an open J-card: her picture as the cover, her name down a cream spine and her details as a track listing with dotted leaders. On a phone the spine lies flat under the cover.
- Striped sunset suns for ratings, a glossy heart with a glint for the favourite and a drop into a pool ripple for the O counter.
- Glossy enamel buttons on a hard shadow, night-blue fields with a turquoise focus ring, pool-lane switches, and navy menus, toasts and modals with the stripe band.
- The Tagger's rows stay flat and still. Scrape dialogs fill the winning toggle with pool water, and StashID links are a pool-blue tab.
- A cassette loader with turning reels, and the empty pool with her line on empty lists.
- A late-night FM host's lines for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Glint, sun and pool-drop bursts on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the dusk and drops the voice.
- Mr Dafoe, Jost and a 16-character subset of Zen Maru Gothic bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts.
