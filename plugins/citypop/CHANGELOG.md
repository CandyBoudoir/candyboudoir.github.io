# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- An airbrushed 1980s dusk after Japanese city pop album covers: a night-blue sky graded into violet with stars, palm silhouettes and a sunset glow, deep navy panels, sunset coral and pool turquoise.
- A striped sun low in the corner that stays down wherever a list, a detail page or the player covers the horizon, so it never sits behind cards, and rises in Settings.
- The navbar carries the J-card stripe band along its foot, with the active page as a sunset sticker and the brand in chrome script. On a phone held upright the band runs along the top of Stash's bottom bar.
- Cards are record sleeves with a printed keyline and a pressed crease. Hover lifts the sleeve, sweeps a sunset across the picture, flashes a glint and shows the catalogue label. The rating is a cream cassette-label sticker beside the checkbox, and progress is an FM dial.
- Audio-only scenes with no cover of their own are a cassette on a pool at dusk, found with one GraphQL request per batch of new cards.
- A performer's page is an open J-card: her picture as the cover, her name down a cream spine and her details as a track listing with dotted leaders. On a phone the spine lies flat under the cover.
- Striped sunset suns for ratings, a glossy heart with a glint for the favourite and a drop into a pool ripple for the O counter.
- Glossy enamel buttons on a hard shadow, night-blue fields with a turquoise focus ring, pool-lane switches, and navy menus, toasts and modals with the stripe band.
- The Tagger's rows stay flat and still. Scrape dialogs fill the winning toggle with pool water, and StashID links are a pool-blue tab.
- A cassette loader with turning reels, and the empty pool with her line on empty lists.
- A late-night FM host's lines for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Glint, sun and pool-drop bursts on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the dusk and drops the voice.
- Mr Dafoe, Jost and a 16-character subset of Zen Maru Gothic bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts.
