# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- A heads-up display in acid yellow and data cyan on matte black, with a faint dot grid and a still scanline behind the page. Panels, cards, menus and buttons have a corner clipped at 45° and frame brackets.
- The navbar is a HUD strip with tick marks along its edge, a reticle beside the brand, a session clock where it fits and the active page in a yellow tab. On a phone held upright it docks at the bottom of the screen.
- Charge chevrons for ratings, a targeting reticle that locks on for the favourite and an overload bolt for the O counter.
- Cards are chamfered panels. On hover the corner brackets close in on the picture, a cyan scan line crosses it once and a readout counts to 100%. The rating is a yellow tag with a five-segment meter, set to the right of the card's checkbox.
- Hovering a button, a tab, a tag or a card title glitches it for 170ms: the type jumps and splits red and cyan. Nothing moves or resizes under the pointer.
- Fields have corner ticks and a blinking block cursor on focus. Delete dialogs carry a hazard-striped header.
- The Tagger's rows stay flat and still. Matches read cyan, warnings yellow and misses red, and StashID links are a cyan tag.
- A segmented loader that counts its percentage up over a scrolling hex dump, and an access-denied lock between hazard stripes on empty lists.
- The voice of the system running the session for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Bursts of pixels, data shards and hex digits on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the display and drops the voice.
- Chakra Petch and Share Tech Mono bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts.
