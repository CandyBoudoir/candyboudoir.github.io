# Changelog

## 0.2.2

Cherry Pick's Your score reads on Overclock, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Cherry Pick's Your score, its guess at how you'd rate a scene, is the rating tag left hollow: black with a yellow frame and yellow type. It had taken the tag's black type over Cherry Pick's dark dim and could not be read.
- Tested beside Insights 2.12.1 and Performers Toolkit 0.17.1.

## 0.2.1

Insights 2.12 and Performers Toolkit 0.13 on the display, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Insights 2.12 and Performers Toolkit 0.13 pick up Overclock's colours by themselves, so with the restyle settings off they already sit on its black.
- With Restyle Insights on, Insights' calendars and its codec and resolution colours take Overclock's signals too. Empty days sit in the dark field; on Insights 2.12 they had vanished into the panel.
- With Restyle Performers Toolkit on, the toolkit's new Stats tab is on the display: its figures and charts are chamfered panels with corner brackets, titles in Chakra Petch and readouts in Share Tech Mono. Its Stats tab before 0.13 is no longer restyled.
- Tested beside Insights 2.12.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.13.1.

## 0.2.0

Insights and Performers Toolkit on the display, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Restyle Insights and Restyle Performers Toolkit settings put those two plugins by another author on Overclock's display: chamfered teal-black panels with frame lines and corner brackets, their names in Chakra Petch, Stash's tabs with a yellow underline under the open one, clipped buttons, cyan links, segmented bars, numbers and labels in Share Tech Mono and a yellow tooltip. Each tab keeps a colour of its own, from Overclock's yellow, cyan, red and magenta and four more signals in orange, green, blue and violet. Insights' heatmaps and its codec, resolution and chart colours keep their own tones.
- Both settings start off, and each shows in Settings > Plugins only while its plugin is on. A change takes effect on the next page you open, without a reload, and so do Calm mode and Plain labels.
- Insights' What works sidebar clears Overclock's taller navbar, and the count on Performers Toolkit's Catalog tab keeps Overclock's tab colours, with the restyle on or off.
- Tested beside Insights 2.11.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.4.2.

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- A heads-up display in acid yellow and data cyan on matte black, with a faint dot grid and a still scanline behind the page. Panels, cards, menus and buttons have a corner clipped at 45° and frame brackets.
- The navbar is a HUD strip with tick marks along its edge, a reticle beside the brand, a session clock where it fits and the active page in a yellow tab. On a phone held upright it docks at the bottom of the screen.
- Charge chevrons for ratings, a targeting reticle that locks on for the favourite and an overload bolt for the O counter.
- Cards are chamfered panels. On hover the corner brackets close in on the picture, a cyan scan line crosses it once and a readout counts to 100%. The rating is a yellow tag with a five-segment meter, set to the right of the card's checkbox.
- Hovering a button, a tab, a tag or a card title glitches it for 170ms: the type jumps and splits red and cyan. Nothing moves or resizes under the pointer.
- Fields have corner ticks and a blinking block cursor on focus. Delete dialogs carry a hazard-striped header.
- The Tagger's rows stay flat and still. Matches read cyan, warnings yellow and misses red, and StashID links are a cyan tag.
- A segmented loader that counts its percentage up over a scrolling hex dump, and a dead scope with a flat cyan trace and No signal on empty lists.
- The voice of the system running the session for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Bursts of pixels, data shards and hex digits on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the display and drops the voice.
- Chakra Petch and Share Tech Mono bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts.
