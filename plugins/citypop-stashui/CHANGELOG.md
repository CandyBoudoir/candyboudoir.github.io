# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release: City Pop as a style for Stash UI.

- City Pop joins the presets under Stash UI's Settings > Appearance > Colors.
- It gives Stash UI City Pop's colours and type. Page and section titles take its brush script on a magenta drop, and the wordmark turns to chrome beside the striped sun.
- Its dusk of stars and palms replaces Stash UI's halftone screen, with a sun that rises only in Settings. Stash UI's own background image takes its place when it is on.
- Colors only, off by default, keeps Stash UI's own fonts, lettering and backdrop. It shows under the presets while City Pop is picked, and under Settings > Plugins.
- Tested beside Stash UI 3.115.1.
