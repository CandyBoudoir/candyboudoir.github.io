# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release: City Pop and Overclock as styles for Stash UI.

- City Pop and Overclock join the presets under Stash UI's Settings > Appearance > Colors.
- A style gives Stash UI the theme's colours and type. City Pop sets page and section titles in its brush script on a magenta drop and the wordmark in chrome beside the striped sun. Overclock sets them in yellow capitals with a glitch split and a reticle by the wordmark.
- Colors only, off by default, keeps Stash UI's own fonts and lettering. It shows under the presets while a style is picked and under Settings > Plugins.
- Tested beside Stash UI 3.115.1.
