# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Insights and Performers Toolkit in leather, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Restyle Insights and Restyle Performers Toolkit settings dress those two plugins by another author in Leather: stitched leather cards, their names in blackletter beside a stud, Stash's tabs with the open one a raised flap with a red edge, dyed leather buttons with blood red for the one that goes ahead, debossed fields and chips with the chosen one pressed into oxblood, leather-strap switches, links with a dashed red underline, and stitched leather tooltips, dialogs and toasts. Their tabs and charts take eight dyes that tell apart on black leather: nickel, blood red, cognac, amber, green, verdigris, steel and plum. Insights' heatmaps and its codec, resolution and chart colours keep their own tones.
- Both settings start off, and each shows in Settings > Plugins only while its plugin is on. A change takes effect on the next page you open, without a reload, and so do Calm mode and Plain labels.
- Insights' What works sidebar clears Leather's taller navbar, and the count on Performers Toolkit's Catalog tab stays readable, with the restyle on or off.
- Tested beside Insights 2.11.0 and Performers Toolkit 0.4.2.

## 0.1.0

First release, for Stash v0.31.1.

- Black pebble-grain leather with saddle stitching on cards, panels, menus, modals and toasts, on a near-black page lit blood red from the top.
- The navbar is a studded leather strap with the active page stitched on in blood red. On a phone held upright the studs run along the top of Stash's bottom bar.
- Pyramid studs for ratings over punched grommets, a studded collar for the favourite and a flogger for the O counter.
- Cards are stitched leather with the picture set in clear; the studs stay on the navbar. The rating is a leather tab with a stud, hung to the right of the card's checkbox.
- Dyed leather buttons with an inner stitch and nickel rivets, fields debossed into the leather with a red stitch on focus, and switches with a nickel snap.
- The Tagger's rows stay flat and still. Scrape dialogs dye the winning toggle blood red, and StashID links are a nickel tag.
- A loader of three studs that light up in turn, and the collar with a short order on empty lists.
- Strict copy for a short list of Stash's messages, matched whole so titles, tags and descriptions are never touched.
- Stud, O-ring and spark bursts on rating, favouriting and the O counter.
- Calm mode and Plain labels settings. Stash's SFW mode keeps the leather and drops the collar and the voice.
- Grenze Gotisch and Oswald bundled in the plugin, since Stash's content security policy blocks font hosts. The leather is a seamless lossless tile at 1x, 2x and 3x, so every screen gets its own pixels.
